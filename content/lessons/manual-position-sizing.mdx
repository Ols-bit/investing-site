Position size is the amount invested in one holding. A larger position means a given percentage fall has a larger effect on your total money. Thinking about possible losses before a trade can make the risk more concrete, but no calculation can predict or cap every real-world loss.

## A simple loss estimate

One basic estimate is position value multiplied by a possible percentage fall. It is an illustration, not a protective guarantee: prices can gap down, trading can be halted, and costs can add to losses. Avoid risking money needed for essential expenses or a near-term goal.

## Worked example

If a position is £500 and its price falls by 12%, the estimated loss is £500 × 0.12 = £60, leaving £440 before fees and taxes. A £1,000 position falling by the same percentage would lose £120. The percentage fall is identical, but the larger position produces twice the loss.
