title: Reading a stock quote

A <Term>stock quote</Term> is a snapshot of trading information. Common fields include the latest price, daily change, percentage change, day's high and low, <Term>previous close</Term> and <Term>trading volume</Term>. Quotes may be delayed, and their meaning depends on the market and the time the data was collected.

## Read the fields together

The latest price is the most recent reported trade, not necessarily the price at which you can trade now. The day's high and low show the range of reported prices during the session. Previous close is the last session's closing price. Volume counts shares traded over a stated period; it is not the number of shares a company has issued.

## Worked example

Suppose the previous close was £40.00 and the latest quote is £41.20. The change is £1.20. The percentage change is £1.20 / £40.00 × 100 = 3%. If the day's high is £42 and low is £39.80, the latest price is within that day's range. It does not tell you tomorrow's direction.
