Investment costs can include dealing charges, platform fees, fund expenses and taxes. A small recurring fee can matter because it is paid from money that might otherwise remain invested. Tax rules vary by country, account and personal circumstances, so treat general examples as illustrations rather than tax guidance.

## Worked example

Suppose an account is worth £10,000 and has an annual fee of 0.5%. A simple one-year estimate is £10,000 × 0.005 = £50. If the account value changes during the year, the actual charge may differ. A transaction fee of £5 on each of 12 monthly trades would add another £60, before any taxes.
