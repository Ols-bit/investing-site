A <Term>Lifetime ISA</Term> is a UK account intended for an eligible first-home purchase or later life. It can hold cash or investments. The account rules include a government bonus, but withdrawals for other reasons may be subject to a charge.

## Contribution and withdrawal rules

In the {ukFigures.taxYear} tax year, the contribution limit is £{formatUKPounds(ukFigures.lisaAnnualContributionLimit)} per year. A government bonus of {ukFigures.lisaBonusPercent}% may be added to eligible contributions, up to £{formatUKPounds(ukFigures.lisaMaximumAnnualBonus)} in that tax year. The contribution limit counts towards the overall ISA allowance.

You must make your first payment before age {ukFigures.lisaLastAgeToOpen}. Contributions and the bonus stop at age {ukFigures.lisaLastAgeToContribute}. The permitted first-home use has conditions, including rules about the property and how long the account has been open. Read the current guidance before relying on the account for a purchase.

## Worked example

If an eligible saver contributes £{formatUKPounds(ukFigures.examples.lifetimeIsaContribution)}, the {ukFigures.lisaBonusPercent}% bonus would be £{formatUKPounds(ukExamples.lifetimeIsaBonus)}. That describes the bonus arithmetic only: it does not account for investment changes, fees, eligibility, or withdrawal charges.

## Rules can change

Tax rules can change and may depend on your circumstances. Check current GOV.UK guidance before making a decision. This lesson is educational and is not personal advice.
