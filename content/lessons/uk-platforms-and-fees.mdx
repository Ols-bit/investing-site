An <Term>investment platform</Term> is a service used to hold an account and access investments. Compare the service, account types, available investments and support as well as its charges. There is no single platform that is right for everyone, and this lesson does not recommend a provider.

## Costs to compare

Look for platform or account charges, fund ongoing charges, dealing fees, foreign-exchange costs, transfer or exit fees and any other transaction costs. Check whether a fee is a percentage, a fixed amount, or both, and how often it is taken. A lower charge does not make an investment suitable or remove market risk.

## Worked example

On an illustrative balance of £{formatUKPounds(ukFigures.examples.platformBalance)}, a {ukFigures.examples.platformFeePercent}% annual platform fee is £{formatUKPounds(ukExamples.annualPlatformFee)}. A separate {ukFigures.examples.investmentChargePercent}% investment charge is £{formatUKPounds(ukExamples.annualInvestmentCharge)}. Together, those example charges total £{formatUKPounds(ukExamples.annualPlatformFee + ukExamples.annualInvestmentCharge)} over a year if the balance stays unchanged. Actual charges and balances can differ.

## Rules can change

Platform services, charges and account rules can change. Read current fee schedules and account terms before making a decision. This lesson is educational and is not personal advice.
